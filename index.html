Last updated: 23 October 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how Aolang Studio ("Aolang Studio," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game **Wanderfin!** (the "Game"). ## Summary We do not operate our own user-account, advertising, analytics, or profiling servers for the Game. However, the Game uses certain Google and Android platform services, including Google Play Games Services for sign-in and cloud saves, Google Play Billing for optional in-app purchases, Android backup and device-transfer services, and Android Health Connect for the optional walking-reward feature. With your permission, the Game reads step-count information from Health Connect to calculate walking rewards and Ritual progress. Raw step totals are processed on your device and are not included in the Game's save files or cloud-save payload. The Game does retain game data derived from your walking activity, such as rewarded days, reward amounts, Ritual completion state, and resulting game currency or progression. This derived game-progress data may be backed up or synchronized through Google services. We do not sell your personal information. The Game does not include third-party advertising and does not use your Health Connect data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or marketing. ## Information We Process Depending on the features you use, the Game may process the following categories of information: - step-count totals obtained from Health Connect with your permission; - game progress, including inventory, currencies, fish and tank data, rewards, progression, gameplay timestamps, and settings; - information derived from walking activity, including rewarded local-calendar days, Step Coin reward records, Ritual progress, and resulting game progression; - a randomly generated installation identifier created by the Game; - your Google Play Games player identifier when Google Play Games features are used; - cloud-save metadata, such as save identifiers, timestamps, revision information, and integrity hashes; - product identifiers, purchase status, and purchase tokens supplied by Google Play Billing during purchase processing; and - technical status and diagnostic information generated locally when the Game or an integrated platform service encounters an error. The Game does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or precise location directly to the Game. We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, messages, call logs, or calendar. ## Health and Fitness Data (Health Connect) The Game includes an optional feature that rewards you with in-game currency called **Step Coins** and may advance in-game Ritual progress based on your real-world walking activity. To provide this feature, the Game requests permission to read step-count records from Android Health Connect. Granting this permission is optional. If you decline, the Game remains playable, but walking-based rewards and progress will not be available. When permission has been granted: - the Game may read your step total for the current local-calendar day; - the Game may read totals for a limited number of recent local-calendar days so that eligible walking can still be rewarded when you did not open the Game every day; - the current default lookback period is seven days, although the configured period may be up to 30 days; - reads may occur when the Game starts and when it returns to the foreground; and - the Game uses the totals to calculate Step Coins, rewarded days, and Ritual progress. The Game does not use Health Connect to write, modify, or delete health or fitness records. The Game is designed to request step-count access only and does not use Health Connect to access heart rate, sleep, weight, location, or other health-record categories. Raw step totals are processed on your device. They are not intentionally included in the Game's persistent save data, Android portable-backup payload, or Google Play Games cloud-save payload. Runtime diagnostic output may be stored temporarily in local application logs on your device. We do not operate a service that receives raw step totals from the Game. The Game does retain data derived from step totals, including: - the local-calendar days for which walking rewards were credited; - the amount of in-game currency already credited for an eligible day; - whether a day qualified for Ritual progress; - Ritual completion and reward status; and - game currency and progression resulting from walking rewards. This derived information forms part of your game progress. If Google Play Games cloud save or Android backup is active, derived walking-related game progress may be stored or processed by Google along with the rest of your game progress. We do not use Health Connect data for advertising, analytics, profiling, creditworthiness, insurance, employment decisions, or any purpose unrelated to providing and maintaining the walking-reward and Ritual features. We do not sell Health Connect data. You can revoke the Game's Health Connect permission at any time through Health Connect or Android settings. Revoking permission prevents future step reads but does not automatically reverse rewards already granted or delete game-progress records previously derived from walking activity. ## Google Play Games Services and Cloud Saves On supported Android devices, the Game uses Google Play Games Services for player authentication and Saved Games functionality. When Google Play Games is available and you are signed in, the Game may receive your Google Play Games player identifier. The Game uses this identifier to associate local cloud-sync state with the correct Play Games account and to avoid synchronizing progress to the wrong account. The Game may upload and download cloud-save data through Google Play Games. Cloud-save data may include: - game progress, inventories, currencies, rewards, and progression; - fish, tank, decoration, plant, and other gameplay state; - gameplay timestamps, cooldowns, and recovery information; - purchase counts recorded by the Game; - derived walking-reward and Ritual progress; - a randomly generated installation identifier; - cloud-save revision identifiers, timestamps, parent-revision information, and integrity hashes; and - other information needed to restore and reconcile your saved game. Device-specific audio settings, Health Connect permission-prompt state, and certain other device-local settings are excluded from the Game's cloud-save payload. Cloud synchronization may occur automatically after local progress changes when Google Play Games authentication is active. The Game may keep multiple cloud-save revisions and recovery points to protect against corruption, migration problems, or conflicting saves. Older recovery revisions may be deleted automatically according to the Game's retention rules. The Game does not receive or store your Google account password. Google processes information used by Google Play Games Services according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies. ## In-App Purchases and Google Play Billing The Game uses Google Play Billing for optional in-app purchases. When you make or restore a purchase, Google processes the transaction and payment information. The Game may receive product identifiers, purchase status, and purchase tokens needed to start, complete, restore, reconcile, or consume the purchase and to grant the corresponding in-game content. The Game may retain a record of purchased pack counts as part of your game progress. The Game does not receive or store your complete payment-card or bank-account details. Purchase and payment information processed by Google is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies. ## Local Storage The Game stores progress and settings in its private application storage on your device. Local data may include game progress, inventories, currencies, settings, gameplay timestamps, derived walking-reward progress, purchase counts, and a randomly generated installation identifier. To reduce the risk of losing progress because of an interrupted or corrupted save, the Game may maintain temporary files, rolling local backup copies, and recovery copies in its private application storage. The installation identifier is a random identifier generated by the Game. It is not based on a hardware identifier, advertising identifier, phone number, or other device fingerprint. It may be stored separately from the main save so that save recovery and cloud-save lineage can be managed safely. It may also be included in Google Play Games cloud-save metadata. ## Android Backup and Device Transfer On supported Android devices, selected game-progress data may be included in Android backup or device-transfer services. The Game creates a portable game-progress copy for this purpose. Device-specific settings and the installation identifier are excluded from that portable backup. Android or Google may retain and restore eligible backup data according to your device configuration, Google account settings, and their applicable policies. As a result, gameplay data may be restored after reinstalling the Game or moving to another device. ## How We Use Information We use the information processed by the Game to: - operate and maintain the Game; - calculate and grant walking-based rewards and Ritual progress; - save, restore, synchronize, and recover game progress; - associate cloud saves with the correct Google Play Games account; - process and restore optional in-app purchases; - prevent duplicate rewards or purchases; - detect save corruption, incompatible save versions, and synchronization conflicts; - maintain the security and integrity of saved game data; and - diagnose technical failures. We do not use information processed by the Game for third-party advertising or cross-app behavioral tracking. ## Information Shared With Service Providers The Game uses the following third-party or platform services: - **Google Play Games Services**, for authentication and cloud saves; - **Google Play Billing**, for optional in-app purchases; - **Android backup and device-transfer services**, for eligible game-progress backup and restoration; and - **Android Health Connect**, for user-authorized access to step-count records. These providers may process information necessary to provide their services. Their processing is governed by their own terms, privacy policies, account settings, and legal obligations. We do not sell personal information. We do not share raw Health Connect step totals with advertisers, data brokers, or our own servers. The Game does not include third-party advertising SDKs. The current version is not designed to use third-party analytics or behavioral-tracking SDKs. ## Data Retention Local game data remains in the Game's private application storage until it is removed through Android application-storage controls, deleted by the operating system, or removed when the Game is uninstalled, subject to Android's retention, backup, and restore behavior. Local backup and recovery copies may remain alongside the active local save until the application storage containing them is removed or they are replaced by newer recovery copies. Google Play Games cloud saves and Android backups may remain after the Game is uninstalled. Their retention is controlled partly by Google, your Google account, your Android device settings, and the Game's cloud-save revision-management behavior. The Game may retain multiple Google Play Games cloud-save revisions for recovery. Older revisions may be pruned automatically, but removing the Game from a device does not guarantee immediate deletion of all copies held through Google services. Google Play may also retain purchase records as required to operate its billing service, maintain transaction history, prevent fraud, or comply with legal obligations. ## Your Choices and Data Deletion You can: - decline or revoke Health Connect step permission through Health Connect or Android settings; - choose not to sign in to Google Play Games, where supported by the platform and Game interface; - manage Google Play Games and Google account information through Google's settings; - clear the Game's local storage through Android's application settings; and - uninstall the Game to remove its active local application storage, subject to Android backup, restore, and retain-data behavior. Clearing local storage or uninstalling the Game may not delete Google Play Games cloud saves, Android backups, device-transfer copies, or Google Play purchase records. These copies may require separate management through your Google or Android settings and may be restored if you reinstall the Game. The current version of the Game may not provide an in-game control that guarantees deletion of every cloud-save revision or platform-held copy. If you contact us regarding a deletion request, we will explain what data, if any, we control directly and which data must be managed through Google or Android. Because we do not operate our own player-account server, we may not be able to identify or delete platform-held information without information and tools supplied by the relevant platform. ## Security The Game uses measures intended to protect save integrity, including private application storage, atomic save operations, integrity hashes, recovery copies, account-binding checks, and cloud-save conflict handling. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Information processed by Google or Android services is also protected according to those providers' security practices and policies. ## International Processing Google and Android platform services may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Any such processing is governed by the relevant provider's terms, privacy policy, and applicable law. ## Children's Privacy The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13.

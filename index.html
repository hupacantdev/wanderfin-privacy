Privacy Policy for WANDERFIN Last updated: 14 September 2026 This Privacy Policy describes how Aolang Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Wanderfin! (the "Game"). Summary We do not operate any servers for the Game and we do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information. The Game can read your step count from Health Connect, with your permission, and uses it only on your device to calculate in-game rewards. Your health and fitness data never leaves your device. Health and Fitness Data (Health Connect) The Game includes an optional feature that rewards you with in-game currency ("Step Coins") based on your real-world walking activity. To provide this feature, the Game requests permission to read your step count from Android Health Connect (the READ_STEPS permission). Specifically: The Game reads your step totals for the current day and for a limited number of recent days (by default, up to the last 7 days) so that walking is still rewarded even on days you did not open the Game. The Game only reads step data. It never writes, modifies, or deletes any data in Health Connect. Your step data is used solely on your device to calculate in-game rewards. It is not transmitted to us, stored on any server, or shared with any third party. Granting this permission is optional. If you decline, the Game remains fully playable; you simply will not earn Step Coins from walking. You can revoke this permission at any time through Health Connect settings on your device. How we use Health Connect data We use the step count read through Health Connect exclusively to determine how much in-game currency to offer you. We do not use it for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to this in-game reward feature. We do not sell or transfer this data to any third party. Information We Collect Apart from the step count described above (which stays on your device), we do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The Game does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location. All game progress and settings are stored locally on your device only. This data is not transmitted to us or to any third party. If you uninstall the Game, this local data is removed. Data Shared With Third Parties The Game does not share any data with third parties. We do not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services in the Game. Data Retention and Deletion Step data read from Health Connect is used transiently to calculate rewards and is not retained by us in any identifiable form. Because all Game data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all data associated with the Game at any time by uninstalling it. You can also revoke the Game's access to step data at any time in Health Connect settings. Children's Privacy The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Permissions The Game may request the following permission: Health Connect – Read steps (READ_STEPS): used only to calculate in-game walking rewards, as described above. The Game requests no other sensitive permissions. It does not access the internet to transmit user data, and does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or messages. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
